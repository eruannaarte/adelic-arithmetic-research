# Primary sources, accepted components, and checked scope

No historical novelty is asserted for interval centering, Gram inversion, Schur elimination, or nuisance projection. The claimed developments are the quantified applications to the frozen complete arithmetic source model.

1. Fredrik Johansson, [Rigorous high-precision computation of the Hurwitz zeta function and its derivatives](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.2877),2013. The paper studies Euler–Maclaurin evaluation with rigorous error bounds. It provides primary algorithmic context for the complete midpoint correction and unwindowed tail evaluation. Here the argument is always Re(s)=2, away from the pole at1. We do not import a theorem about arithmetic recovery from this paper; the full Dirichlet-product identity and its hypotheses are proved in PROOFS.md.

2. Fredrik Johansson, [Arb: Efficient Arbitrary-Precision Midpoint-Radius Interval Arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02831),2017. Arb's enclosure arithmetic is a trusted computational component. Every published consequence uses outward rational endpoints and fails on nonfinite or insufficiently separated balls. The192/256-bit repeats and independent direct calculations are controls, not a formal proof of the numerical library implementation.

3. FLINT's official [complex zeta API documentation](https://flintlib.org/doc/acb_dirichlet.html#riemann-zeta-function) and [complex-number API](https://flintlib.org/doc/acb.html) describe the automatic zeta evaluator and its wrapper. The recorded installed version is FLINT3.6.0; current online documentation may be newer. The package trusts the installed evaluator's enclosure contract rather than claiming to reconstruct its internal algorithm from scratch. Every exact positive-time input and the complete finite head are enclosed, including rational rounding of the final vector.

4. Samuel Rosa and Radoslav Harman, [Optimal approximate designs for estimating treatment contrasts resistant to nuisance effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06079),2015, Statistical Papers2016. This primary work treats additive nuisance effects including time trends and conditions for resistant designs. It is context for the established nuisance-design viewpoint, not an imported optimality theorem for our complex Dirichlet, integer-envelope problem. Our weights and source tails do not instantiate its treatment-contrast hypotheses; the exact block-inverse argument needed here is fully proved locally.

5. The accepted project premises are the previously fully reconstructed finite and remote certificates cited in README.md and their proofs in ARITHMETIC_SENSING_V.md, ARITHMETIC_SENSING_V_END_TO_END.md, and research/five_paths_2026_09/path3_noise/PROOF.md. Their fixed degree14, retained cutoff50, finite cutoff10^6, times, nested sample indices, exact weights, and remote binding are revalidated by the imported strict source checker. New correction and nuisance-channel computations are rebuilt in this chain. Trusting unchanged complete tails is explicitly distinguished from rebuilding new calculations.

The elementary mathematical tools used here—absolute convergence, unique prime factorization, finite-dimensional Hermitian bounds, the Neumann series, Cauchy–Schwarz, and two-block elimination—are proved or derived in the local complete proof. No external optimality, recovery, or novelty theorem is silently imported.
