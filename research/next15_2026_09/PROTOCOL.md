# Fifteen adaptive research cycles

User objective: continue the five research paths for at least fifteen substantive “what next” cycles, choosing each successor from the preceding result, then report findings and conclusions.

The work uses five linked sequences, one for each prior path, with three completed cycles required per sequence. Independent sequences run in parallel. Within each sequence, the next objective is recorded only after the preceding objective has yielded a supported result. The final global ledger will identify both cycle numbers and dependency links; parallel sequences are not represented as a single chronological experiment.

A counted cycle must contribute a complete mathematical argument, a complete numerical certificate tied to its defining model, or a useful proved obstruction. A pilot, failed sufficient test without an informative result, extra test run, documentation task, or a cosmetic improvement does not count. Every cycle records its question, baseline, result, evidence, limits, and the next question justified by that result. The final sequence successor is recorded as a future question, outside the fifteen completed cycles.

Initial questions come from the previous report: sharper harmonic acquisition obstructions; preparation robustness and improved design; arithmetic bias/noise optimization; richer actual-field query geometry; and target-placement or bandwidth robustness for resolution transfer. Later questions are not fixed in advance.

Preserve research/five_paths_2026_09 and all earlier sources. New work belongs here. Use accepted theorem citations with explicit hypothesis checks, or supply complete elementary proofs. Exact and outward numerical arguments require independent consequence checking and reconstruction of decisive numerical premises. Model assumptions, timing correlations, actual reused readings, metric normalization, and infinite tails remain explicit. Do not infer physical validation or historical novelty from successful calculations.
