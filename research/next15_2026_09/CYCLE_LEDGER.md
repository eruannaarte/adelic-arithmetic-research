# Fifteen completed adaptive research cycles

Numbering is reading order, not global chronology. The work was organized as five independent three-cycle chains, with overlapping work across agents; all five did not run simultaneously. Within each chain, the next objective was recorded after the preceding result had been supported and validated; each final successor below is a future question, not a sixteenth completed cycle. The [protocol](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/PROTOCOL.md) defines substantive progress. Optimization pilots, repeated checks, documentation, and repaired checker defects are not counted separately. The [evidence index](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) distinguishes complete proofs, numerical reconstruction, inherited premises, and independent controls.
