## Mathematical status and retained trust

**Harmonic:** H1's finite classification is exact integer arithmetic plus a proved pair bound. H2's universal horizon exclusion needs the complete interval cover, not its discovery grid. H3's exact equality follows from the proved contraction/self-map lemma and the actual interval Jacobian, not a small residual. Ordinary cycle checkers reconstruct all decisive new quantities;256/384-bit replay and independent formulas corroborate them. Trust remains in the inspected mathematics and outward Arb elementary functions. [Sources and scope](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/harmonic/SOURCES.md).

**Preparation:** The three complete finite inverse/calibration proofs use a fixed rational left inverse and shared unknown launch errors. Consequence checking verifies exact incidence, clock semantics, costs, domains, enclosures, BA=I, and rational gates. Source-to-reading validity additionally requires replay of the 31-coordinate nonlinear ODE tubes. Teschl's smooth-flow hypotheses are checked through positivity of the mechanical mass determinant and accepted finite tubes; the first-exit enclosure argument is supplied locally. Arb's elementary/power-series bounds are trusted. LP/SLSQP provide candidates only; P3's global statement concerns its fixed certificate polytope, not all physical designs. [Proof and primary references](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/preparation/PROOFS.md).

**Quadratic:** Q1/Q2 have analytic arithmetic proofs, explicit field witnesses, and exact finite checks. Their metrics are normalized coefficient norms. Actual finite realizability uses the correct quadratic ring of integers, ideal factorization and splitting rules, CRT, and Dirichlet's theorem in coprime progressions; finite examples do not prove infinitude. Q2's complete tail and nonattainment arguments establish the infinite endpoint result, without an efficient-decoder claim. Q3 returns to the specified physical sensor and uses complete d2 tail/Gram premises. Its fresh 497,500-term reconstruction is stored separately from the final rational decision. The [quadratic proof](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/quadratic/PROOFS.md) identifies accepted primary inputs and their hypotheses; [baseline realizability proof](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/five_paths_2026_09/path4_realizability/PROOF.md) supplies the full finite realization construction.

**Noise:** N1's necessary interval is about a declared complete sufficient certificate, not actual worst-bias impossibility. N2 uses the full zeta^14 envelope; every digital correction entry is freshly enclosed and its approximation error charged. N3 reconstructs the actual nuisance cross map, supplies a complete new unwindowed-tail channel, and validates N2's supplied correction. Complete d14 finite/remote baseline bounds remain explicitly inherited from prior full reconstructions, with strict parameter and formal bindings rechecked. Hashes identify premises; they do not prove them. Noise covariance is unchanged by N2's deterministic correction; N3 changes the linear decoder. Exact-solve guarantees do not supply hardware or finite-precision implementation validation. [Sources and trust](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/noise/SOURCES.md).

**Resolution:** All new locality, first-moment, operator-loss, and centroid bounds are analytic plus exact rational arithmetic. Their central finite and continuum floors remain inherited interval premises whose complete rational consequences are rechecked automatically. The original model-to-Arb Taylor reconstruction retains its documented prior verification. The full million-state examples are explicitly floating regressions, not proof inputs. R1/R2 use full-field output; off-center targets generally activate odd modes. R3 changes the operator and adds a narrower target prior. Direct 99-row acquisition requires spatial-average readout access, and every row retains the global x-average. [Proof and scope](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/resolution/PROOFS.md) · [validation record](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/next15_2026_09/resolution/VALIDATION.json).

The package does not assert historical priority for standard methods. It distinguishes proved new applications, sufficient-bound improvements, exact obstructions, and diagnostic numerical examples. Independent audits supplement the proofs; they are not proof-assistant or empirical hardware verification.
