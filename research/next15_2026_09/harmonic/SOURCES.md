# Primary sources, comparison, and proof dependencies

The three new applications are deliberately scoped to the exact finite factor-product acquisition model. Their elementary mathematical arguments are supplied in PROOFS.md; no source below is treated as a proof of a new numerical witness.

- Ankur Moitra, [*Super-resolution, Extremal Functions and the Condition Number of Vandermonde Matrices*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1681), 2014 preprint / STOC2015. The primary abstract relates cutoff, frequency separation, and recoverability in a sparse-frequency model. This establishes relevant prior context for frequency-gap obstructions. Its estimator and separation theorem are not imported here: our normalized distances are between known-prime product-factor sources, and Cycle1 proves its two-response inequality directly.
- Laurent Demanet and Nam Nguyen, [*The recoverability limit for superresolution via sparsity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01385), 2015. The primary source analyzes deterministic-noise minimax scaling for sparse Fourier recovery and connects it to restricted Fourier conditioning. It reinforces that restricted source-pair geometry and noise amplification are classical concerns. We do not transfer its minimax rate to the present product-simplex model or claim a new general superresolution theorem.
- Siegfried M. Rump, [*Verification methods: Rigorous results using floating-point arithmetic*](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/rump/intlab/ActaNumerica2010.pdf), *Acta Numerica*19(2010),287–449; [publisher record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/acta-numerica/article/abs/verification-methods-rigorous-results-using-floatingpoint-arithmetic/770FE58E5293985CCAB770AF09C4F3FF). Theorem13.3, on PDF page89, states a preconditioned derivative-box inclusion criterion for a unique nonlinear zero, including nonsingularity of the preconditioner. Its continuously differentiable square-map and contained-box hypotheses apply here. Cycle3 proves the stronger explicit norm-contraction criterion it uses in full, so this source establishes attribution rather than filling an omitted proof. The contribution is the model-connected interior collision, not a new general interval-Newton theorem.
- Fredrik Johansson, [*Arb: Efficient Arbitrary-Precision Midpoint-Radius Interval Arithmetic*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02831), IEEE Transactions on Computers66(8)(2017),1281–1292, and [the author's inclusion-semantics documentation](https://fredrikj.net/arb/using.html). These describe the numerical arithmetic contract trusted here. Exact integer ratios, exact rational times, and rational preconditioners are converted through fmpq. Finite nonzero-width intervals carry transcendental and matrix arithmetic uncertainty; floating displays do not decide acceptance.

Cycle1 uses unique prime factorization, finite exact enumeration, and an elementary trigonometric identity. Cycle2's weighted-minimum inequality and whole-interval cosine enclosure require no imported optimization-duality theorem. Cycle3 proves its fixed-point assertion by an explicit Cauchy/geometric-series argument; its polynomial map is smooth globally and its strict box and derivative inequalities are reconstructed. Thus there is no unstated nontrivial arithmetic existence theorem or topological result imported into these three cycles.

Trusted components remaining are Python exact integer/Fraction arithmetic and python-flint0.9.0 / FLINT3.6.0 / Arb elementary functions, complex operations and matrix arithmetic. A matrix inverse proposes a rational preconditioner; only its subsequently checked contraction inequality has proof authority. Search used NumPy/SciPy but is not a proof dependency. The 384-bit rerun and alternative raw-model expansions are useful controls, not independent implementations of Arb.

The earlier baseline [Path2 proof](/Volumes/KINGSTON/Vibecoding/Math/research/five_paths_2026_09/path2_harmonic/PROOFS.md) supplies the comparison target and earlier cubic bound. Its full reading-count theorem and global sufficient schedules remain untouched. The new results do not prove shortest times, unknown-clock recovery, optimal noise rates, or any arithmetic infinite-tail statement. This targeted primary-source check supports attribution and scope; it is not an exhaustive novelty review.
