The degree-eleven worker independently reviewed `index.html`, `core.js`,
`app.js`, the exported evidence and its generating code. The final reviewed
version has no identified mathematical or scope error. This is an internal

## The elementary example is an exact, observation-specific answer set

For the shared-offset model, subtraction gives `2=x+e2−e1`; the feasible
interval is exactly `[2−2epsilon,2+2epsilon]∩[0,4]`. Conversely, every member
has an explicit witness: set `e2=(2−x)/2`, `e1=−(2−x)/2` and
`u=5−x−e1`. The two stated observations follow and both errors respect the
allowance. With a single reading or independent unrestricted offsets, any
source value is feasible by choosing its offset(s). This establishes the full
continuous statement; the loop over slider cases is a code check of that
proof, not a substitute for it.

The interface now explicitly distinguishes the integer source class
`{0,1,2,3,4}` from the real source class `[0,4]`. It therefore does not imply
that merely asking an integer question restricts a previously continuous
source. Integer membership tests use integer hundredths before conversion to
displayed endpoints. The text correctly limits the result to observations
5 and 7 and does not advertise a uniform noise threshold for every possible
observation.

## The compact theorem states the required signs and joint incidence

The pair weights `c_j,c_k` are explicitly positive. Combined with the strict
pair Gram inequality, the weighted Cauchy–Schwarz argument is valid. The
source error follows from the stated positive nominal Gram floor and the
separately bounded query inverse direction. Shared timing/model parameters
remain shared within each explanation; competing explanations may choose
different allowed parameters. The nonlinear remainder is not omitted when
vertex convexity is used for linear directions.

The processing guide qualifies projection as removal of a **linear nuisance
subspace**. It does not assert that an arbitrary nonlinear uncertainty image
can be projected out with the same guarantee. The complete proof links
supply the fuller nuisance, approximation and acquisition hypotheses.

## Certified badges use containment, not floating plots

Spatial controls are percentages of a fixed published box. Decreasing any
radius preserves the original guarantee; exceeding any active limit changes
the display to outside the certificate. In the known-clock/potential bank,
those radii are identically zero and the corresponding controls are disabled.
The stated source class, readout averages, source-relative measurement norm,
and nominal/true time restriction remain explicit.

The strict spatial percentages now round **upward**. This corrects a review
finding: ordinary fixed-decimal formatting had rounded the six-row bound down
while retaining a strict `<` symbol. The final six-row joint display uses
`<0.085284%`, which is a safe presentation of the certified upper bound below
0.0852831068%. The known-time bank likewise rounds safely. The full-box error
is retained when sliders decrease; the lab does not invent a tighter matrix
certificate from those changes.

Arithmetic badges inherit only two proven families: degree twelve with
amplitude at most 4,000 and clock multiplier at most 120, or degree eleven
with amplitude at most 500 and multiplier at most one. Both retain the fixed
frequency, mismatch, sensor and future normal-residual contract. The browser
neither verifies a new observation nor infers family membership from a small
residual. The latter premise is stated next to the accepted status.

The plotted clock coefficients already contain the coefficient inverse gain;
the drift component adds that gain once. Complete tail, sensor, mismatch,
centering and residual contributions are all present. The odd/even Taylor
channels are complete, including the degree-eleven parity distinction and
orders 33/34. These are floating **display estimates**, not outward interval
computations. Out-of-contract messages therefore describe the displayed
estimate and withhold a certificate; they do not infer a rigorous obstruction
from a floating sum or claim impossibility beyond a saved box.

The arithmetic text now explicitly says that the coordinate error is a
complex-modulus bound and integer rounding applies to the real part. The
amplitude selector changes its declared family, not an independently inferred
calibration. The degree-eleven heading describes a **bound** missing the
specified target; its failed sufficient certificate is not described as a
universal degree lower bound.

## The five paths remain distinct

The guide includes preparation/calibration, harmonic acquisition, arithmetic
query structure, multiscale arithmetic sensing and finite-resolution sensing,
with local evidence targets for all five and the nine-package progression.
Their connections to the common query framework retain the relevant limits:
no performed apparatus calibration, whole-source harmonic coverage rather
than endpoint checks, explicit arithmetic realizability questions, complete
infinite tails, and error measured against information strength. The current
two-model consequences are not represented as physical validation of all five
programmes or as independent external peer review.

The lab's offline execution and share-state mechanism do not alter any
mathematical contract. Public release reachability, final asset hashes and
production deployment are responsibilities of the separate publication
validation and Website Manager handoff.
