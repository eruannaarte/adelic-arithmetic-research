# Objective, result, and next question

**Starting point.** The preceding harmonic H3 proved one exact interior collision at a six-time schedule whose product vertices were well separated. It did not establish whether the collision survived changes in the acquisition times.

**Objective chosen before the new certificate.** Prove a substantive family of full-source obstructions by a parameter-uniform contraction over an explicit nonzero box of time perturbations. Keep the original labelled product model and metrics. Quantify both interior-source separation and the vertex guarantee over the whole time box.

**Development decision.** A fixed source center leaves a first-order residual in the time perturbation. A rational affine predictor in twelve source coordinates cancels this to sufficient accuracy. Integrating the predictor's complete parameter derivative supplies a rigorous moving-center residual bound. The proposed radius was reduced until both factored and independent integer-label interval reconstructions passed, then frozen at time radius 1/40000 and root radius 1/30000. This radius is not claimed optimal.

**Accomplished.** Every schedule in the six-dimensional time box has vertex floor greater than 1.03 and an exact interior collision with probability floor 0.07 and factor distance greater than 0.79. The collision preserves two fixed values of the probability query in the prime-3 factor, proving noiseless answer diameter greater than 0.5496 and estimator error at least 0.274800677541277801. The exact collision branch is Lipschitz in the times.

**Controls and limits.** Four complete reconstructions (two algebraic forms, two precisions) and ten focused tests pass. The same fixed root box with zero affine predictor fails the self-map gate: its radius ratio exceeds 2.55. An attempted time radius 1/1000 also fails this sufficient certificate. Neither failure establishes the inverse problem outside the proved region. No physical acquisition experiment was performed.

**Evidence-based successor, not yet executed.** Find an extra reading with a uniform response gap over the entire certified collision branch, then examine whether it also supplies a full-source guarantee rather than only removing this branch. A second option is validated continuation to enlarge the forbidden design region. Both follow from the fact that the obstruction is now an explicit uniform family rather than one pair at one schedule.
