# Objective: separated spatial rows for the joint target/source query

The preceding package certifies consecutive asymmetric banks with 50 rows retaining 99% of the original information floors, or 48 rows retaining 97%. The next objective is a genuinely separated bank of individually selected rows, for the same 21 target indices, two source modes, known continuous time interval [1,2], finite grid n=1001, and declared source/noise norms.

A new bank will not inherit a centroid decoder across arbitrary gaps. Instead, certify the entire two-dimensional image for every target and all 4-column maps formed by pairs of different targets. A strictly positive pair margin must separate the complete target/source uncertainty tubes. Individual target floors then control least-squares source error.

The source model, global x-average per row, and output normalization remain unchanged. Numerical proposals are discovery only. Complete finite-generator reconstruction, continuous-time remainders, any periodic/infinite transverse approximation, and numerical decoder error must be charged explicitly. A failed sufficient bound will not be called nonidentifiability.

The initial target is to preserve the existing source-relative sensor allowances, 3e-7 in L2 or 3e-8 in either H1 calibration, with fewer than 48 directly acquired rows. If a stricter noise budget is necessary, its cost will be stated. The common transfer interface is: row restriction, target-specific two-column map, pair-block Gram floor, operator approximation radius, sensor radius, individual inverse floor, and exact time-cell coverage.
