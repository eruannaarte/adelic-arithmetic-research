Audit the proposed transfer of query guarantees through restriction, nuisance removal, finite approximation, and numerical computation. Identify necessary and sufficient exactness conditions, the correct retained-noise geometry, minimal directional budgets under shared uncertainty, and counterexamples to unjustified compositions. Require a theorem with model-derived margins and concrete application consequences beyond tautological containment.

The starting sources are the preserved unified answer-set proofs and the original OIG/arithmetic/atlas integration. Work in this review directory, preserving earlier research and application producers. Review the new framework's proofs and rational consumer independently as they become available. Mathematical novelty is not assumed; primary optimal-recovery and positive-operator references provide context only.

The resulting review distinguishes: exact retained-noise lifting; implementability of a restricted full quotient versus reconstruction of the entire quotient; fixed-query saturation versus source saturation; conditional answer sets versus uniform minimax risk; shared-stage support versus independent error budgets; exact compact-convex separation versus nonlinear query unions; and amplitude-independent nuisance elimination versus numerical leakage.

Acceptance consists of complete mathematical review, explicit exact witnesses, independent constrained-minimum calculations, and an audit of the executable sign and rank conditions. Passing finite checks supplements the proofs and is not represented as a new research theorem or new physical application.
