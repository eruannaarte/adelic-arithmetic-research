The same seven spatial-average channels now certify **|g−4/5| ≤ 2.5e−6**
in the physical potential V_i=1+g x_i, with shared clock error **5e−7**, sensor
radius **1.1e−7**, and relative source error **below 0.000801020**. The claim
covers every target 490–510, every nonzero two-mode source, and all nominal
and true times in [1,2]. No channels were added and the source-accuracy target
remains 0.001.

The previous stated generic generator allowance ||H'−H||≤1e−9 implies only
|g−4/5|≤2.5e−10 using ||L_y tensor diag(x)||≤4. The new stated potential
allowance is **10,000 times larger than that previous allowance**. This
compares declared sufficient profiles, not optimal tolerances; the previous
norm-sum inequality itself has some unused headroom. The improvement applies
to this specified potential family and cannot be transferred to every
unstructured generator perturbation of the same operator norm.

The main mathematical gain is preserving uncertainty through the nominal
inverse. If all model disturbance is first converted into a scalar output
radius and then amplified by the weakest source singular value, this same
rectangle fails the 0.001 source-accuracy gate. The actual clock/potential
directions have much smaller inverse influence. Certifying their joint
matrix action first proves the stronger result. The two unknown scalars are
shared across readings within one explanation; competing target explanations
are allowed different admissible scalar values.

The joint corner calculation also gives smaller bounds than adding the
separately certified direction norms: 6.6e−8 versus 7.2e−8 in observation
space, and 2.8e−5 versus 2.9e−5 after inversion. These reductions concern
these certified upper bounds; they are not claims about the exact maxima.

The potential derivative is reconstructed from the defining finite-x
semigroup, using differentiated noncommuting matrix recurrences. A complex-time
Cauchy estimate controls the complete omitted derivative Taylor series.
Separate Laurent-image and finite-boundary walk bounds connect the auxiliary
periodic computation to the actual 1,002,001-state finite model. The
clock/potential cross remainder is charged explicitly. An independent
consumer checks **2,688 target/time cells**, using centered polynomial
composition rather than the producer's direct interval evaluation.

Twelve raw rational synthetic observations were generated by **direct
exponential reconstruction at the true perturbed potential and time**, rather
than by the nominal linearized model. A separate 320-bit reconstruction
reproduces all readings and their full-model sensor promises. The operational
raw decoder recovers all twelve targets and sources across source scales
1e−8, 1 and 1e8. Its largest observed relative source error is below
**1.719406e−5**. The full potential derivative kernel independently reproduces
its 1,782 exported coefficient intervals at 384 bits.

These computations establish the stated mathematical guarantees. They do
not establish apparatus calibration or measured membership in the potential
family. The six-channel acquisition question is a separate study; this
comparison deliberately retains the established seven channels.
