## Scope corrections that must accompany summaries

A positive vertex floor is not a full-source guarantee. A failed sufficient bound is not an impossibility theorem. The earlier >127× midpoint-correction improvement concerns a decoder's certified noise allowance: known invertible centering does not increase intrinsic information. Infinite coefficient-space thresholds and actual finite-sensor bounds use different metrics. Continuous query diameter is not generically twice optimal estimation radius. A known a(1), complete tails, shared nuisance incidence, physical velocity units, target/source restrictions, and numerical-error budgets must not disappear when results are summarized.

The existing pendulum chamber and atlas provide synthetic numerical validation, not measured apparatus calibration. Smaller spatial banks save acquisition only when the specified spatial-average readouts are directly available.
